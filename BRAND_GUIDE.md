# Brand guide

Verde Arena is the definitive brand for this football ecommerce project. The brand should feel modern, athletic, professional, and elegant, with enough personality to stand apart from generic marketplaces.

## Brand name

| Item | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Definitive name | Verde Arena |
| Meaning | A football-first brand that connects pitch culture, premium sportswear, and a refined green visual identity. |
| Brand role | Storefront brand and main ecommerce identity. |

## Name alternatives

These names are not the selected identity, but can be useful for future collections, campaigns, or private labels.

| Alternative | Possible use |
| --- | --- |
| Campo Verde | Training or grassroots collection. |
| Arena Once | Premium eleven-player matchwear line. |
| Linea de Gol | Campaign or editorial section. |
| Golden Eleven | Limited edition collection already planned in the catalog. |
| Northline FC | Urban training sub-brand already planned in the catalog. |

## Slogan

Primary slogan:

> Built for the match. Designed for the game.

Secondary lines:

- Football gear with matchday attitude.
- Train sharper. Play cleaner.
- Modern football essentials, curated for every match.

## Brand personality

| Trait | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Athletic | Feels active, focused, and performance-oriented. |
| Premium | Uses restraint, hierarchy, and detail instead of visual noise. |
| Confident | Speaks clearly and directly without overpromising. |
| Modern | Uses clean layouts, strong typography, and intentional color. |
| Approachable | Feels useful for real players and fans, not only elite athletes. |

## Communication tone

The tone should be direct, confident, and helpful.

Use:

- Short, active sentences.
- Product benefits connected to football use.
- Clear ecommerce language.
- Confident CTAs.

Avoid:

- Marketplace-style urgency overload.
- Empty hype.
- Corporate SaaS language.
- Overly casual slang.
- Fake scarcity unless the product state supports it.

## Design principles

| Principle | Application |
| --- | --- |
| Sport first | Every major visual decision should reinforce football, movement, or match readiness. |
| Premium restraint | Use color, spacing, and typography with control. Do not overload sections. |
| Commerce clarity | Product name, price, stock, and CTA must remain easy to scan. |
| Own the palette | Sea Green, Ebony, Golden Sand, Mauve Shadow, and Lavender Mist define the identity. |
| Editorial moments | Hero, featured products, and premium sections should feel campaign-like. |

## Visual direction

The UI should combine clean ecommerce structure with sports-brand energy.

Visual cues:

- Large editorial hero sections.
- Strong product photography.
- Calm product grids.
- Golden Sand used as a focused highlight.
- Mauve Shadow used for premium depth.
- Sea Green used for active brand energy in light mode.
- Dark mode with a deeper premium hierarchy, not a light-theme inversion.

## Photographic style

| Context | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Hero | Editorial sports imagery with motion, texture, and confident composition. |
| Product cards | Clean product images with consistent crops and brand-tinted neutral backgrounds. |
| Product detail | Gallery with close-ups for material, grip, stitching, sole, and fit details. |
| Category cards | Lifestyle or action-oriented crops that communicate the category quickly. |
| Admin/future uploads | Require consistent aspect ratios and avoid licensed club marks. |

Photography should avoid generic stock-photo energy. The goal is curated, athletic, and brand-owned.

## Iconography style

Use simple line icons with consistent stroke weight.

Direction:

- Rounded but not playful.
- Minimal internal detail.
- Works at small sizes.
- Uses brand tokens for active states.
- Supports ecommerce states such as shipping, security, returns, cart, filters, and stock.

Avoid:

- Filled icon sets mixed with outline sets.
- Cartoon sports icons.
- Default dashboard icon styling as the main identity.

## Recommended typography

| Role | Recommendation | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headings | Sora, Space Grotesk, or Archivo | Strong geometric forms with modern sports energy. |
| Body | Inter or Geist | High readability for ecommerce and UI text. |
| Numbers/prices | Same family as body, medium/semibold weight | Keeps price hierarchy clear without feeling like a discount marketplace. |

Initial recommendation:

- Headings: Sora.
- Body/UI: Inter.

## Logo proposal

The logo should be a wordmark-first identity:

- Text: `Verde Arena`.
- Mark concept: a minimal shield or pitch-line monogram using `VA`.
- Geometry: subtle football pitch angle or center-circle reference.
- Color: Sea Green in light contexts, Lavender Mist or Golden Sand accents on dark/premium contexts.

The logo should work without a ball icon. That matters because literal football logos can become generic quickly.

## Logo applications

| Application | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Header | Horizontal wordmark, compact height, strong readability. |
| Footer | Full wordmark with slogan or short brand statement. |
| Favicon | `VA` monogram or simplified pitch-mark. |
| Product cards | Avoid repeating the logo on every card; let product imagery breathe. |
| Packaging mockups | Use wordmark plus Golden Sand accent line. |
| Social previews | Wordmark over Mauve Shadow or Sea Green background. |
| Dark theme | Use light wordmark with Golden Sand or Sea Green accent. |

## Brand consistency checklist

- [ ] The UI feels like a sports brand, not a marketplace clone.
- [ ] Primary CTAs use brand hierarchy, not default framework colors.
- [ ] Product imagery follows consistent crops and background rules.
- [ ] The logo remains readable in header, footer, favicon, and social contexts.
- [ ] Copy is confident and useful without fake urgency.
