## Commercial benefits

| Benefit | Short copy | Supporting copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Curated football gear | Football-first selection | Products organized around how players and fans actually shop. |
| Matchday-ready quality | Built for repeat play | Apparel, boots, balls, and accessories selected for training and match routines. |
| Clear product details | Know before you buy | Size, stock, category, and product information stay easy to compare. |
| Fast shopping flow | Less friction, more focus | Browse, inspect, and plan your purchase without unnecessary account steps. |
| Future secure checkout | Payment-ready roadmap | Checkout and payment integrations are planned only after the UX is validated. |

## Trust messages

Use these across benefits, product detail, cart, and checkout planning:

- Secure checkout planned for the payment phase.
- Mock checkout for UX validation only.
- Clear stock status before purchase.
- Curated football products with consistent product details.
- Easy product comparison across categories.
- No account required for the first browsing experience.

Do not imply real payment processing, live inventory, or shipping guarantees until backend integrations exist.

## Content rules

- Be direct and useful.
- Keep CTAs action-oriented.
- Avoid fake urgency.
- Avoid promising real payments, delivery, inventory, or account features before backend exists.
- Prefer football-specific language over generic ecommerce filler.
- Keep UI text in English unless the product direction explicitly changes.
